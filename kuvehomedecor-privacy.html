Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Kuve Studio is a private desktop content management application developed for managing social media and ecommerce content for Kuve Home Decor.

Purpose of the Application

Kuve Studio assists the account owner with creating, preparing, organizing, and transferring home textile marketing content to supported platforms. The application is intended for private use by the owner of Kuve Home Decor and is not offered as a public service.

TikTok

Kuve Studio may connect to TikTok only after explicit authorization by the TikTok account owner.

The application may use TikTok developer services to prepare and upload content to the authorized TikTok account for review and publishing.

TikTok authorization credentials and access tokens are used only to provide the requested integration with the authorized account. They are not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Pinterest

Kuve Studio may connect to Pinterest only after explicit authorization by the Pinterest account owner.

The application may use Pinterest APIs for functions such as:

  • Preparing and creating Pins
  • Uploading Pinterest images
  • Reading and managing authorized Boards
  • Organizing Pinterest content

OpenAI

Kuve Studio may use OpenAI services to assist with generating visual content and marketing metadata such as titles, descriptions, alt text, and content classification.

Data Handling

Kuve Studio does not sell personal information. Data accessed through connected services is used only for the functionality requested by the account owner.

Authentication credentials and API tokens used by the desktop application are intended to be stored locally and are not intentionally published or shared with third parties.

Kuve Studio does not process or store customer payment information.

Third-Party Services

Use of TikTok, Pinterest, OpenAI, Etsy, and other third-party services is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of those respective services.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Kuve Studio features, integrations, or applicable platform requirements change.
